Explore Tamil Nadu — 12 destinations

From lively cities and ancient monuments to hill stations, waterfalls and peaceful beaches, choose a destination for your next trip.

Ooty

Enjoy cool weather, tea gardens, the Nilgiri Mountain Railway and scenic viewpoints.

Best for: Hills · Nature · Families

Kodaikanal

Discover Kodaikanal Lake, Coaker's Walk, pine forests and misty viewpoints.

Best for: Lakes · Trekking · Relaxation

Madurai

Explore Meenakshi Amman Temple, heritage streets and famous local cuisine.

Best for: Heritage · Temples · Food

Chennai

Visit Marina Beach, museums, historic neighbourhoods and cultural venues.

Best for: City life · Beaches · Culture

Mahabalipuram

See the Shore Temple, Pancha Rathas and remarkable Pallava rock-cut monuments.

Best for: UNESCO heritage · Architecture

Thanjavur

Discover the Brihadeeswarar Temple, art, bronze work and Chola history.

Best for: History · Temples · Art

Rameswaram

Experience Ramanathaswamy Temple, Pamban Bridge and coastal scenery.

Best for: Pilgrimage · Sea views

Kanyakumari

Watch the sunrise, visit the Vivekananda Rock Memorial and enjoy the coast.

Best for: Coastal views · Sightseeing

Yercaud

Take in the Eastern Ghats, Yercaud Lake, coffee plantations and forest roads.

Best for: Quiet hill getaway · Nature

Valparai

Travel through tea estates, rainforest landscapes and scenic mountain routes.

Best for: Tea estates · Wildlife · Drives

Hogenakkal

See the dramatic Cauvery River waterfalls and rocky river scenery.

Best for: Waterfalls · Day trips

Tiruchirappalli

Visit Rockfort Temple, Srirangam and important heritage landmarks.

Best for: Heritage · Temples · City break
